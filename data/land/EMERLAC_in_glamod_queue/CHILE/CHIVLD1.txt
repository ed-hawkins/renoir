Observers: Sr. Carlos Anwandter (1 April 1801 Luckenwalde, 10 July 1889 Valdivia) a pharmacist that emigrates to Chile at 1850. The observatory was in his house as well as the first brewery in Valdivia founded by him (Bernedo Pinto, 1999)/  Bernedo Pinto P. 1999. Los industriales alemanes de Valdivia 1850-1914. Historia 32, 5-42. 			
Location Observatory: -39.81, -73.24
